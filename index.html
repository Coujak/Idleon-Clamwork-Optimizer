Game-data costs

Next upgrade costs are calculated automatically from your imported levels, current Worker Class, Scurgeworks discounts and the game's verified cost-growth constants. Break-even cost is the maximum worth paying for a single production upgrade before saving becomes faster; discount upgrades are assessed across purchase sequences.

AFK PEARL FARMING

Pure Pearl reroll comparison

What-if calculator

When your AFK claim offers a reroll, the strategy is to keep rewards with Pure Pearls and reroll misses. Rerolls replace the previous claim; they don't add another payout. You only get the button when its actual conditions are met.

Compare one character’s AFK claim
Enter AFK claim values to compare rerolling Pure Pearl misses with collecting immediately.

This is a simplified expectation model, not a native AFK simulator: it assumes replacement claims have the same normal reward, Pure Pearl chance and Pure Pearl value as the first claim. Actual reroll offers depend on talents, guild bonuses, food, and auto-claim. Claim timing and replacement drop rates can also differ. This calculator does not feed speculative values into the upgrade recommendation.
