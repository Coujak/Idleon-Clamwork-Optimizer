PEARL UPGRADES

When should I stop investing?

Game-data costs

Costs use verified game data. The search looks ahead through multiple Pearl purchases, then compares their total payoff to saving for the next promotion. A break-even cost is the highest worthwhile single-upgrade price under the chosen farm-rate assumptions; discount upgrades require multi-purchase evaluation.

UpgradeLevelNext costBreak-even costOne-buy time impact

The promotion search can test up to 16 future purchases, including Frugality, Anti Inflation and Clam Comrades. Multi-Scalping is evaluated only when participating characters have known multikill. Extra clams do not always mean proportionally more kills; choose the conservative setting if your map is already kill-rate limited. Absolute time estimates require observed production.

Simulation milestones

What is estimated versus verified?
  • Verified: Pearl upgrade prices, Worker Class promotion prices and chances, current saved upgrade levels
  • Modeled: next-promotion purchase sequences and unspent Scurge Point allocation
  • Calibrated: absolute Pearl and EXP rates only when supplied or derived from measured kills/hour
  • Not yet verified: future reset-cycle kill rates, active combat mechanics and native AFK reroll rewards
